# Bachelor of Business Scholarship - RMIT University

## Bachelor of Business Scholarship
Offered to new students enrolling in the Bachelor of Business degree, the Bachelor of Business Scholarship is awarded to the applicant who has achieved outstanding academic performance in high school.

## Overview
The scholarship is available exclusively to new students who have achieved outstanding academic performance in high school and will be enrolling in the [Bachelor of Business program ](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/study-at-rmit/undergraduate-programs/bachelor-business)at RMIT Vietnam.
| Campus  | Number of scholarships and value  |
| --- | --- |
| Saigon South campus  | 01 x 50% of the respective Bachelor’s degree program  |
| Hanoi Campus  | 01 x 50% of the respective Bachelor’s degree program  |
## Eligibility
To be eligible for this scholarship, you must:
  * Be a citizen of Vietnam;
  * Have completed high school with a minimum grade 12 GPA of **8.5/ 10.0** ; or IB diploma with a minimum of 33 points in total; or GCE ‘A’ Levels (UK) of minimum 11 points for three Advanced subjects (A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1), or equivalent;
    * Your high school graduation date must be within two years of the scholarship application deadline.
  * Have IELTS (Academic) 6.5+ (no band below 6.0) or equivalent. For other international English entry requirements, please view the [English equivalency requirements](https://www.rmit.edu.au/study-with-us/international-students/apply-to-rmit-international-students/entry-requirements/english-requirements) (links to Australian website);
    * Your IELTS test result must still be within its two-year validity period on the start date of your intended intake semester in 2026.
  * Have applied for admission to the [Bachelor of Business program](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/study-at-rmit/undergraduate-programs/bachelor-business) at RMIT Vietnam and meet the program entry requirements (conditional offer may be considered);
  * Be commencing study in the respective Bachelor’s degree program in 2026. If successful, the scholarship will only apply from semester 3 2026 (October 2026).

## Selection criteria
  * Past academic performance will be assessed by the selection committee and ranked according to the eligibility criteria. The top-ranking applicants of each campus (1 at the Hanoi campus and 1 at the Saigon South campus) will be awarded the scholarship.
  * Past academic performance is weighted as follows: 60% GPA Year 12 + 30% GPA Year 11 + 10% GPA Year 10.
  * For Vietnamese high school transcripts that do not include a yearly cumulative GPA, the Scholarships Office will calculate the GPA for each year by averaging the GPA of the subjects assessed on the 10-point scale. Subjects assessed as pass/fail will not be included in the calculation.
